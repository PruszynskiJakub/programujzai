I deleted 30 minutes of work today.
Had a system prompt dialed in almost perfectly. 
Closed the tab. Gone.
But I still had the output saved.

So I asked Claude: 

"Here's what this prompt generated and here's the original purpose. Reconstruct the prompt."

Pasted the output and the purpose.
Got back a prompt that was 90% identical to what I'd lost.

This works for: 
- Recovering your own deleted prompts 
- Learning from outputs you admire 
- Understanding why something worked

Reverse prompt engineering. Underrated skill.
