Stop getting better at prompting. You're solving the wrong problem. Be great at thinking.

Watch an expert work. They don't dive into Claude with clever instructions.

They pause. They ask:

What does success actually look like?

What problem is underneath the problem?

What would I tell a junior dev before they started this?

Then the prompt writes itself.

Meanwhile most devs iterate for an hour because they skipped the 5-minute question:

"Do I even know what I want?"

That's the gap. Not syntax. Clarity.

Experts don't have better tools. They have better questions.
What's the 5-minute question you keep skipping?
