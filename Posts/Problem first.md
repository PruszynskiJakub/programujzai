I spent 2 hours last week generating the same image.
26 variations. At the end none of them right.

Then I stopped and asked: "What problem am I actually solving?"

Turns out I was prompting for "professional" when I needed "scroll-stopping."

Wrong problem = infinite iterations.

Now I don't write a single prompt until I answer:

What does success look like? (Specifically.)

Who is this for and where will they see it?

That 2-minute check saves hours.

Most prompting advice focuses on technique. 
The real skill is knowing what you're actually asking for.
