- Summary: Reader is a powerful reading app that helps you save, highlight, and take notes on many types of documents using easy keyboard shortcuts. It works on web, mobile, and with a browser extension that lets you highlight web pages and YouTube videos. Reader also offers features like text-to-speech and organizes your reading into a Library for saved items and a Feed for automatic content.
